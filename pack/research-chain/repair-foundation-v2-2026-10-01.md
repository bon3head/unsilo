# ARTIFACT 3 — THE SESSION PROTOCOL v2

## Operating contract

One worker at a time, in one sequential chain. No parallel repair tracks, simultaneous blocker owners, or unreviewed merges of competing baselines.

Each session receives one bounded primary assignment plus the exact dependencies it must inspect. An assignment may require multiple sessions. More sessions are allocated only when unresolved acceptance criteria, adversarial failures, recovered authority, or integration defects require them.

This round establishes and verifies semantic contracts. Production implementation, migrations, deployment, and build authorization are outside its authority. Non-runtime design fixtures and explicitly identified research experiments may support semantic acceptance only within their stated evidentiary limits.

The governing authority track is **RECONSTRUCTED-AUTHORITY** unless a future recovery-impact review explicitly and successfully moves an affected claim onto ORIGINAL authority. No worker may relabel a reconstructed contract, fixture, question, assertion, glossary, or experiment as original/recovered.

The remaining repair sequence after Foundation v2 is:

1. **Foundation-v2 admission on entry to the next fresh session.** The next worker first verifies this exact Foundation-v2 candidate against R01–R16 and the self-pin. If rejected, no downstream semantic work may rely on it.
2. **B4 — temporal interval algebra:** completion semantics, invalid/empty admissibility, relation oracles, effective/knowledge time, RestrictTime, obsolete-P3 retirement, dependency preservation.
