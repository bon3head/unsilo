## Bound<T> frozen (abstract; representation stays 7R)
Bound = KNOWN(v) | UNBOUNDED | UNKNOWN.
- KNOWN(v): established at the evidence's own granularity; not arbitrary precision.
- UNBOUNDED: affirmative source semantics of no finite bound; never from a missing field.
  "Until filled" ≠ unbounded.
- UNKNOWN: finite bound may exist but unestablished; never −∞/+∞/now/first/last-obs.
- Point times (publication, capture) permit KNOWN/UNKNOWN only — never UNBOUNDED.
- Three time categories not interchangeable even if all render as ISO strings.
- Aligns with temporal literature's determinate/indeterminate time distinction
  (Jensen/Snodgrass; consensus glossary).
