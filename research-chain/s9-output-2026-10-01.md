- DirectComparable(r1,r2) ⟺ PredFamily, G, ScopeShape, Ke, Kk, P, Ξ all equal
  AND members of declared Π. Structural predicate; no similarity threshold.
- Four statuses: DIRECT (all agree) / VERSION_AWARE_VALID (pins differ but B3
  satisfied, bridges explicit) / CONDITIONAL (non-pin mismatch; shown with
  mismatch vector, cannot feed B9 Difference) / NOT_COMPARABLE (B3 fails —

## B3: version-aware comparison
- Pin vector P=(S,V,C,R); ΔP=(ΔS,ΔV,ΔC,ΔR). Each changed axis adjudicated
  independently; VersionCompatible = CompatS∧CompatV∧CompatC∧CompatR. One failed
  changed axis → INVALID (never "approximate").
- ΔV: explicit bridge covering the vocabulary elements actually used, or INVALID.
  (Label matching, analyst "obviously same", silent synonym tables ≠ bridge.)
- ΔC: explicit bridge covering every classification value contributing to the
  result; partial coverage → INVALID for out-of-scope values. No "mostly
  comparable".
- ΔS: field-compatibility proof (same relevant fields, types, temporal roles,
  claim semantics under both schemas) OR explicit schema bridge. Unrelated
  addition can pass the proof; rename/retype/role-change needs a bridge. "Small"
  is irrelevant.
- ΔR: same durable identity under both registries, or evidence-backed continuity
  proof. CIK rename (same CIK → renamed A) = continuous; CIK reassigned to
  unrelated B = NOT_COMPARABLE + registry-integrity anomaly for real SEC data.

## Bridge semantics (formal v1 artifact)
- Bridge B: (source versions, declared domain) → (target versions, declared
  semantic frame). Frozen identity+version; explicit endpoints; explicit mapping
  scope; evidence backing; own frozen bridge pin vector (SB,VB,CB,RB); closed
  B10 dependency closure; bitemporal validity (a bridge not known at Kk can't
  serve that cut; outside effective scope can't apply retroactively);
  fail-closed scope (unmapped input → invalid, never guessed).
- NOT: analyst judgment, silent alias table, runtime fallback, undocumented
  cleaning, one-off exception, presentation relabeling, implicit transitive chain.
- No silent composition: B2→3 + B3→4 ≠ admissible B2→4 unless the composed
  mapping is itself frozen as a dependency with scope/provenance.
